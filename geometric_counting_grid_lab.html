3. Challenge

A 4 × 6 grid has how many rectangles of any size?

There are 5 horizontal grid lines and 7 vertical grid lines.

Choose two of each, multiply, then check by setting the grid dimensions to 4 rows and 6 columns.

New notation

C(5, 2) means choose 2 items from 5: 5 × 4 ÷ 2 = 10.

C(7, 2) means choose 2 from 7: 7 × 6 ÷ 2 = 21.
